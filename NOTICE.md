# Data, software and publication provenance

The controlled modern-eye experiments use ODA/ODA-3D outputs and should be
read alongside the original project and methods paper:

- Currea et al., [ODA source code](https://github.com/jpcurrea/ODA), MIT licence.
- Currea et al. (2023), *Communications Biology*,
  [ODA-3D methods paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-023-04575-x).

The fossil volumes were obtained from the public Humboldt-Universität zu
Berlin data deposit associated with the *Archegonus* study:

- [Fossil compound-eye dataset](https://doi.org/10.18452/20002).
- Schoenemann et al. (2019), *Nature Communications*,
  [A 429-million-year-old compound eye](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-10459-8).

The fossil anatomy literature contains active disagreements about the identity
of some deeper structures. The image-domain boundary detected here is therefore
reported as a candidate internal CT boundary unless independent anatomical
evidence is supplied.

The modern population-prior experiment uses the public InSegtCone bee scan and
published layer label:

- Tichit et al. (2022), *BioData Mining*,
  [InSegtCone](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40850-021-00101-w).
- [MorphoSource project 000377798](https://www.morphosource.org/projects/000377798),
  raw *Apis mellifera* media `000396182`.
- [InSegtCone source and labels](https://github.com/zhoutunhe/InSegtCone).

The raw MorphoSource media are not redistributed here. Users must obtain them
from MorphoSource and follow the media-specific download agreement and citation
requirements.
