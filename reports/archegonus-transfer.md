# Frozen transfer to Archegonus

The *Archegonus* volume provided a useful independent imaging test, although
the public archives may represent different parts of the same eye rather than
independent animals.

The outer stage transferred cleanly. Seventy-six persistent facet centres were
found, with approximately 70.1 micrometre spacing and a nearest-neighbour
lattice coefficient of variation of 0.112.

The internal stage did not transfer under the frozen *Asaphus* criteria:

- only 1 of 76 facets passed the original quality gate;
- the candidate edge was indistinguishable from inter-facet controls
  (AUC 0.506, p = 0.458);
- a clearly labelled post-hoc relaxed analysis retained 64 facets but improved
  over the radial baseline by only 1.6%.

This is recorded as a negative transfer, not as a prompt for further tuning.
The surface-facet detector generalised to this volume; the internal-boundary
reconstruction did not.
